Mona Lisa

Leonardo da Vinci

Year of Creation: 1503–1506 (approx.)

The Mona Lisa is one of the world's most famous paintings. Created by the Italian artist Leonardo da Vinci, it is admired for its mysterious smile, realistic details, and remarkable artistic techniques. Today, it is displayed in the Louvre Museum in Paris, France, and attracts millions of visitors every year.
