The Kiss

Gustav Klimt

Year of Creation: 1907–1908

The Kiss is one of Gustav Klimt's most celebrated masterpieces. Painted during his Golden Period, it portrays a couple embracing in a field of flowers, wrapped in luxurious golden robes. The artwork symbolizes love, intimacy, beauty, and emotional connection through its rich decorative style and brilliant use of gold leaf.
