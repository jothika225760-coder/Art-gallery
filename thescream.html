The Scream

Edvard Munch

Year of Creation: 1893

The Scream is one of the world's most famous expressionist paintings. Created by Norwegian artist Edvard Munch in 1893, it portrays a figure standing on a bridge with an expression of fear and anxiety beneath a dramatic, swirling sky. The painting symbolizes human emotion, loneliness, and psychological distress.
