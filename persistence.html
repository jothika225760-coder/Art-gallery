The Persistence of Memory

Salvador Dalí

Year of Creation: 1931

The Persistence of Memory is one of the most famous surrealist paintings created by Spanish artist Salvador Dalí. The artwork shows melting clocks in a strange dream-like landscape, representing the fluid nature of time and memory. It is admired for its imaginative symbolism and detailed style.
