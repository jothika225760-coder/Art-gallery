Girl with a Pearl Earring

Johannes Vermeer

Year of Creation: 1665

Girl with a Pearl Earring is one of the most celebrated paintings by the Dutch artist Johannes Vermeer. Often called the "Mona Lisa of the North," the artwork is admired for its lifelike expression, beautiful lighting, and the large pearl earring that gives the painting its name. It remains one of the most famous portraits in the history of art.
