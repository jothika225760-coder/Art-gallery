Starry Night

Vincent van Gogh

Year of Creation: 1889

The Starry Night is one of Vincent van Gogh's most famous masterpieces. Painted in 1889 while he was staying at an asylum in Saint-Rémy, France, the artwork depicts a swirling night sky above a peaceful village. The bold brush strokes, bright stars, and expressive style make it one of the most recognizable paintings in history.
